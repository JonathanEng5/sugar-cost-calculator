# sugar-cost-calculator
This free, interactive calculator reveals the hidden health costs of Coca-Cola consumption —can by can, year by year.

By modelling annual soft drink intake against sugar content and established health research, the tool translates personal habits into:

Total sugar consumed (kg)
Increased risk of Type 2 diabetes
Estimated lifetime health costs (direct and indirect)
Whether you're an educator, policymaker, health advocate or simply concerned about your own habits, this tool makes the true cost of sugary drinks visible—moving beyond vague nutrition labels to expose tangible personal and societal consequences.

Grounded in robust public health data, the calculator is designed to:

Inform personal choices
Strengthen public health campaigns
Support evidence-based policy change
Because once we know the cost, we can choose better—for ourselves and for society.
